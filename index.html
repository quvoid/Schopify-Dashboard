Silver jewellery · what the competition is running on Shopify

A read of the public Shopify storefronts of GIVA, Palmonas and The Amethyst Store, seen through a paid media lens: the prices, offers, launch pace, stock and hero products that every one of their Meta and Google campaigns lands on.

Product feeds read 15 Oct 2026. Silvana is shown only as a reference line, using our internal estimates.

01 · Price architecture

Where each store's ads can enter the funnel

The entry price (10th percentile) is the cheapest believable hook a prospecting ad can lead with. The median is where most of the catalog sits once someone clicks.

02 · Offer pressure

How much discounting the ads are carrying

The share of SKUs with a compare-at price, and how deep the cut is. That tells you how far each brand's ads depend on "% off" messaging.

03 · Catalog freshness

New SKUs going live: fuel for catalog and DPA ads

Products created in each of the last 12 weeks. Frequent drops keep Advantage+ catalog ads and "new in" retargeting fresh, while a flat line leaves the same creatives running.

04 · Ad traffic at risk

Sold-out share by category

When a category is out of stock, collection-page and catalog ads pointing at it send paid clicks to products nobody can buy.

05 · Merchandising signals

What the product tags say they are pushing

The share of SKUs carrying each kind of tag. Tags drive collections, filters and catalog product sets, so they show which themes each brand can build ad sets around.

06 · Side by side

Landing-page and conversion stack

What a paid click finds after the ad: depth of range, imagery and checkout tooling.

07 · On the shelf now

Hero products live today

The latest in-stock products each store tags as bestsellers, or its newest drops where it has no bestseller tag. These are the SKUs most likely to show up in their catalog ads this week.
